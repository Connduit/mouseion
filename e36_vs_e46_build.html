BMW Build Plan: E36 328i vs E46 330i

A classy, OEM+ BMW sleeper with a BMW engine: raw and analog to drive, understated to look at. Prices are rough ballparks and vary by region, year and condition. Verify current prices before budgeting, and plan for 20-30% over.

Build goals

  • Raw, analog feel: manual transmission, hydraulic steering, minimal electronics
  • Strong, seat-pinning acceleration; beating a C8 Z06 is no longer a requirement
  • BMW engine only; LS and other non-BMW swaps are reference only
  • Quick and stable at high speed, used hard only on canyon roads, drag strips and track days
  • Classy OEM+ look, not flashy; no cage or race seats
  • Total cost stays below the price of the sports cars it rivals

Base car: E36 328i vs E46 330i

The E36 is lighter, rawer and cheaper to maintain; the E46 is stiffer with the stronger M54. Figures are approximate and from memory.

E36 328i (US 1996-98)E46 330i (US 2001-05)
Stock power~190 hp~225 hp (ZHP ~235)
Weight~3,050-3,150 lb~3,300-3,450 lb
FeelLighter, rawer, hydraulic steeringStiffer, more refined, hydraulic steering
ChassisFlexes more; needs seam welding, bushings, bracingStiffer from the factory
Known issuesCooling parts, rear subframe mounts, rustRear subframe cracking, VANOS seals, plastic cooling parts
Swap supportLarge kit catalog; fewer S62 or modern-BMW kitsGood support; more electronics to work around
Parts and maintenanceGenerally cheaper, DIY-friendlyGenerally higher
Purchase priceRising; clean cars carry a premiumOften cheaper than a same-condition E36; verify listings
Keep-the-engine potentialM52 limited at big powerM54 is a stronger base
  • Pick the E36 for the lightest, rawest car if you will do much of the work yourself.
  • Pick the E46 for a stiffer, more forgiving base and a stronger stock engine.
  • Long term the E36 is usually cheaper to maintain and modify.
  • Parked idea: E46 GTR-style widebody; it clashes with the understated brief unless toned down.

Build order and costs

Fix weaknesses first, then handling and braking, then power last.

Phase 0: Buy the car (~$5,000-9,000)

  • E36: 1996-1998 328i, manual (1995 was the 325i, also a usable shell)
  • E46: 2001-2005 330i, manual; ZHP (about 2004-2006) is best balanced but costs more; confirm by VIN and option codes
  • Mileage sweet spot about 100k-180k; over 200k only if very cheap
  • E36 prices: project ~$2,500-5,000; solid ~$5,000-9,000; very clean ~$10,000-20,000+. E46: check listings
  • Manual, clean title, no structural rust; pre-purchase inspection ~$150-250

Phase 6: Engine and supporting systems

See engine options. Fuel (Walbro pump, injectors), engine management (BMW adapter harness and ECU, or Haltech/MaxxECU), cooling, swap hardware, labor and a dyno tune.

Phase 7: Aero, exhaust and final tune (~$500-2,000)

Subtle M3-style lip or small ducktail; catback by Supersprint, Eisenmann or Active Autowerke; final dyno tune and alignment.
