The long-term garage

Cars I Want

A garage built gradually around driving feel, DIY projects, performance, track days, drifting, and year-round practicality. These are roles and candidates—not a promise to buy every car.

The Garage

Each car should have a purpose. The goal is to build the collection over time instead of buying everything at once.

BMW E36 328i — Driver / Drift Project

Strong contender

The older BMW platform that keeps pulling me back. A manual E36 328i could be a hands-on project with a great balance of steering feel, back-road fun, and room to develop drifting skills.

  • Prioritize a solid, rust-free chassis and mechanical health first.
  • Build in stages: maintenance, tires/brakes, suspension, alignment, then power.
  • Keep it street-usable and focused on driver feel rather than chasing numbers.

BMW E46 — Alternative BMW Project

Strong contender

The other older BMW generation I really like. Compare it directly with the E36 for condition, price, steering feel, practicality, and how much work each example needs.

  • Look for a clean, well-maintained example over the cheapest listing.
  • Check common age-related cooling, suspension, and chassis issues before committing.
  • Choose based on the actual car—not just the generation.

Fun Daily / Winter Daily

Role to fill

A car I enjoy owning and driving every day—not a disposable beater. It should be dependable enough for regular use, fun on back roads, and capable in New England weather with appropriate tires.

  • Golf R and Focus RS are appealing all-weather performance options.
  • An E36 or E46 has a strong emotional pull, especially if I can find a good example and care for it properly.
  • Manual, responsive steering, and an engaging engine matter; winter usability matters too.

Dedicated Driver's Car — Canyon / Track

Role to fill

A car chosen primarily for the feeling behind the wheel: steering feedback, balance, and confidence on curvy roads and at track days. Raw driver involvement comes before outright speed.

  • Manual transmission preferred.
  • Coupe or sedan is fine.
  • Miata, Corvette, and Porsche are not the target for this slot.
  • Could overlap with the BMW project if it does the job well enough.
Steering feelTrack daysCurvy roadsDriver focused

Power Car — Roll Racing / Acceleration

Future goal

A separate build for serious straight-line acceleration. This slot can prioritize power and performance without forcing the daily or driver's car to compromise its personality.

  • Open to RWD or AWD and different engine layouts.
  • Build around a realistic total budget, reliability, and supporting modifications.
  • Keep high-speed performance to legal, controlled environments.
AccelerationRoll racingPower buildLong-term

Dedicated Drift / Project Car

Future goal

A running, driving starting point that can be modified and repaired without worrying about keeping it pristine. It should be street-usable, but its main purpose is learning and improving at drifting.

  • Manual transmission required; RWD strongly preferred.
  • Prioritize parts availability, DIY repairability, and realistic running costs.
  • Keep it separate from a nicer daily if drifting starts consuming parts and time.

Truck / Utility Vehicle

Future goal

A normal-height vehicle for winter weather, snowboarding trips, hauling people and gear, and occasional motorcycle transport. Practicality matters, but modification potential is a plus.

  • Ford F-150, Toyota Tacoma, and Toyota 4Runner are the current shortlist.
  • Automatic is fine; keep stock or sensible ride height rather than lowering it.
  • Motorcycle hauling without a trailer is a bonus, but a trailer is acceptable for rare trips.
WinterUtilitySnowboardingHauling

Rally-Style Winter / Daily Car

Optional direction

A lower-cost, durable car that can handle rough weather and spirited use while still being cared for. Prefer exploring non-Subaru options too, rather than defaulting to the obvious answer.

  • Should remain at a practical ride height.
  • Look for parts support, reliability, and affordable repairs.
  • This could overlap with the fun daily instead of becoming another separate car.
